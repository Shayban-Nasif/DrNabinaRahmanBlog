Published Research

Original Research Article

Evaluation of early surgical and functional outcomes of anterolateral thigh free flap (ALTFF) and radial forearm free flap (RFFF) for reconstruction of soft tissue defects of oral cavity

Journal: International Surgery Journal | Nov 2025; 12(11): 1933-1939

Key Findings: This study highlights that both ALTFF and RFFF are reliable for reconstruction, with ALTFF showing high survival rates (90.9%) and satisfying functional outcomes in the Bangladeshi population.
